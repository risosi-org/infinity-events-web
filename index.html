FAQ

Good questions.

Is Infinity Events a licensed event company in Dubai?

Yes. Infinix Infinity Events holds a Dubai professional licence (No. 1659104, Commercial Register 2939797) for Events Organizing & Managing, and is part of Infinity Group UAE.

What types of events do you manage?

Corporate events and conferences, award shows and gala dinners, weddings and private celebrations, brand launches, concerts, business summits, exhibitions and international roadshows.

How early should we book?

For large galas, award nights and summits we recommend 8–12 weeks. Smaller corporate and private events can often be delivered in 2–4 weeks depending on venue availability.

Do you handle venues, permits and vendors?

Yes. We manage venue sourcing, stage and AV production, décor, catering coordination, entertainment, photography and videography, guest management and the relevant event permits.

How do I get a quote?

Use the Event Planner above, WhatsApp us on +971 58 931 0114 or email info@infinixinfinityevents.com. We reply with a tailored proposal within 24–48 hours.
